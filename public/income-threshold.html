Circuit 02 - Income Threshold

Prove your income.
Without revealing it.

Uses a Poseidon commitment to bind the proof to your specific income without revealing the value. The circuit verifies income >= threshold and Poseidon(income, blinding) = commitment.

private: income private: blinding_factor public: threshold public: commitment
1 - Commitment
2 - Proving
3 - Result
1
Compute your commitment

The commitment is Poseidon(income, blinding_factor) - a hash that proves you know a specific income without revealing it. Compute it once and share it with the server during onboarding.

private Your income
private Blinding factor (any large random number)
public Your commitment - Poseidon(income, blinding_factor)
2
Generate and submit proof

The proof verifies your income meets the threshold and matches your commitment. Your actual income never leaves the browser.

How it works: snarkjs runs the circuit locally with your private income and blinding factor. The server only receives the proof and public signals - threshold and commitment.
public Minimum income threshold
public Commitment (auto-filled from Step 1)
