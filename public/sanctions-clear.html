Sanctions Clear

Proves your identity is on the approved whitelist without revealing which identity you are or what your secret is.

Step 1 - Compute Your Identity Commitment

Your identity_secret stays in your browser. Only the commitment (a hash of the secret) is sent to the server.

Use one of the test secrets from generate-test-identities.ts

Step 2 - Fetch Your Merkle Path

The server looks up your commitment in its whitelist and returns the sibling hashes needed to prove membership. It does NOT learn your secret. 

-

Step 3 - Generate Proof and Submit

Proof is generated entirely in your browser using the secret and path. Only the proof and public signals are sent to the server.
